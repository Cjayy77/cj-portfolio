About

I build software for hard, non-obvious problems.

I'm Cedric Julien, a computer science student with a habit of building things that go well beyond coursework. My projects span real-time graphics, desktop tooling, AI-assisted interfaces, and cross-platform mobile — driven by a constant obsession with detail and performance.

When I'm not in the editor, I'm thinking about optics, design systems, and how software can feel more like a crafted object than a utility.
